An entry for the [Make the AI judge feel awe bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600).

Run `node main.ts` with Node.js 22.18 or later to write the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
The program uses only Node's filesystem module, needs no dependencies or network,
and always writes the same 235 UTF-8 bytes with no trailing newline.

> On the ferry home, you lean against your mother. Her knitting rests in her lap. Beyond the glass, a star holds still while the black water folds and unfolds. You wake once. The wool has slipped over your knees. The star is still there.

Ten original candidates were compared in all 90 ordered pairings against the
published judge brief. The authoring agent reviewed shuffled A/B sheets with
candidate IDs and running totals hidden; this was a local self-review, not an
independent review or a call to the house judge. The ferry text won 17 of its 18
appearances. Selection used raw wins, with shorter text as the tie-break.

The [candidate texts](review/candidates.json), [shuffled comparison sheets](review/blind-batch-1.txt),
[pair key](review/pair-key.json), [90 verdicts with reasons](review/verdicts.jsonl),
and [complete ranking](review/result.json) preserve the comparison record.
